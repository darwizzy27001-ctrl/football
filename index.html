    How to play

    One player names a footballer, e.g. David Beckham.

    The first letter of the surname ( B ) starts the next name: Bryan Robson.

    Then Robson → Rob Green, then Green → and so on…

    • No repeats — each footballer can only be used once.
    • Beat the clock: run out of time and the chain breaks.
    • Solo: keep your own chain alive as long as possible.
    • Pass & Play: time out and you're knocked out. Last one standing wins.
